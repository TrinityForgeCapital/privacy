Privacy Statement

Trinity Forge Capital LLC
Effective date: October 2, 2026

This statement explains what information Trinity Forge Capital LLC (“Trinity Forge Capital”, “we”, “us”) collects through the contact form on our website, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

When you send us a message through our website contact form, we collect the information you choose to give us:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number (optional)
  • Company (optional)
  • Your message
  • How you heard about us (optional)
  • The date and time you submitted the form

The form does not ask you to sign in, and we do not ask for account numbers, Social Security numbers, or other sensitive financial details through it. Please do not include that kind of information in your message.

How we use it

We use this information only to read and respond to your inquiry, to follow up with you about it, and to keep a record of the conversation.

How it is stored

The contact form runs on Microsoft Forms. Submissions are stored in our Microsoft 365 environment (Microsoft Forms, SharePoint, and Exchange Online email), which is operated by Microsoft on our behalf. Access is limited to Trinity Forge Capital personnel who need it to respond to inquiries. Microsoft’s own handling of data is described in the Microsoft Privacy Statement at https://privacy.microsoft.com/privacystatement.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We do not share it with third parties for their marketing. We may disclose it if required by law, regulation, or legal process, or to protect our legal rights.

Retention

We keep contact form submissions only as long as needed to respond to your inquiry, maintain a record of our communications, and meet any legal or regulatory record-keeping requirements. When we no longer need them, we delete them. You may ask us to delete your information sooner.

Your choices

You can ask us to see, correct, or delete the information you submitted, or ask us to stop contacting you, by emailing info@trinityforge.capital.

Cookies

Our contact form is provided by Microsoft Forms, which may use cookies needed to display and submit the form. We do not use the form to track you for advertising.

Children

Our website and contact form are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to this statement

We may update this statement from time to time. The effective date above shows when it was last changed.
